## Core principle

> *"Alle Dokumente müssen nachprüfbar sein: Interne Dokumente, Interviews und Auswertungen gehören in den Anhang"* (Formale Anforderungen 2026)

> *"Zitierfähig sind grundsätzlich nur solche Quellen, die vom Leser nachvollzogen und überprüft werden können."* (FAQ)

Since TFs are about your employer, internal sources are normal. They are only acceptable if the examiner can **see** them, which means the appendix. The examiner receives nothing except the TF PDF and the Selbstreflexion.

## Internal documents

- In principle unpublished texts (internal documents, lecture scripts) are *not citable* (grey literature). **Exception:** explorative or qualitative studies such as **case studies**, *"sofern wesentliche Ergebnisse auf internen Quellen beruhen"*. TFs usually fall under this exception.
- After agreeing with the company, attach **the specifically cited section** as a copy in the appendix and refer to it in the text.
- Bibliography format suggested by the FAQ: `AUTOR/HERAUSGEBER, Jahr. Titel: Zusatz zum Titel. Ort: Herausgeber. Internes Dokument` — e.g. *CONTINENTAL AG, 2014. Geheime Unternehmensstrategie. Hannover: Continental AG. Internes Dokument* (fictitious). Adapt it to your chosen style.
- Internal sources go into the **Quellenverzeichnis**, together with everything else (no separate categories).
- Examiner: *"Interne Quellen nicht einsehbar"* is a typical 3,7–4,0 deficiency in the Zitation criterion.

## Confidential data (FAQ)

1. Agree **in advance** with the Betreuer which internal data you need and what may go into the appendix.
2. Set the **Sperrvermerk** checkbox at the Auftragsklärung upload if needed. It is binding, and data does not leave NAK.
3. If data still cannot be included: **redact** it, show **excerpts**, or obtain the information through **expert interviews** that you can then cite.
4. If something cannot be included at all: add a **footnote** at the relevant place in the text saying that, after agreement with the company, inclusion was not possible. Also inform the examiner via the **Selbstreflexion**, and optionally contact the Team Transferleistungen in advance. This should be the exception, *"nur in begründeten Fällen"*.

## Expert interviews (FAQ, verbatim essentials)

- *"Es gibt keine Vorlage und keine Beschränkung, wie viele Interviews Sie durchführen oder in welchem Umfang."*
- **Transcripts in the appendix are mandatory.** They do not have to be verbatim; *"auch Stichpunkte sind zulässig"*.
- Each protocol must contain **Gesprächsdatum, Gesprächspartner:in, Funktion/Position im Unternehmen, Thema des Interviews**, and be **signed by the interviewee**. Tipps p. 3 also says *"Interviewprotokolle (unterschreiben lassen)"*.
- **Do not name yourself** in the protocol (double-blind).
- Interviews belong in the **4-week writing period**. Contacting interviewees in advance is recommended.
- Refer to the interview in the text, especially to back up internal company data.
- **Experteninterviews are listed in the Quellenverzeichnis.**
- NAK library recommendation for interviews and questionnaires: an e-book on ProQuest Ebook Central (docID 4443072).
- Examiner: if the Selbstreflexion mentions expert conversations, they must also be **cited in the TF and documented** as a Gesprächsprotokoll. Otherwise it is unclear which statements are your own.
- Examiner: anything stated without a source counts as subjective perception. *"zur Not auch ein kurzes (anonymisiertes) Gesprächsprotokoll"* is better than nothing.
- Examiner (positive): mention in the introduction that an interview was conducted to connect theory with practice, and document it properly.

## Lecture scripts, earlier TFs

- **Lecture scripts:** grey literature, not citable. Use them to find primary sources. If unavoidable, add a footnote like *"Vorlesung am 20.02.2020 von Prof. XY im Modul X"*, with the exact location, and get the lecturer's permission.
- **Earlier TFs and papers:** attach them in the appendix to make them citable (*"Anhang X, Transferleistung 6, S. xx"*). Use primary sources for fundamentals. **No copied passages** (self-plagiarism counts as Täuschung).
