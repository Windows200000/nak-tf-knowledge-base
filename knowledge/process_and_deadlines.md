## What a TF is

- **6 Transferleistungen**, 30 ECTS in total, written during the practice phases over 6 semesters (Tipps p. 1). They are the link between study and the training company and prepare for the BA thesis.
- Workload is **150 h per TF**: 50 h in the company (getting into the topic, finding it, discussing with experts), 60 h research outside the company, 40 h writing (FAQ "Workload"). At submission you must enter a realistic workload value. It is a mandatory field and has **no** influence on the grade.
- Goal (Tipps p. 1): *"Erkenntnisgewinn, also Einordnung, Erweiterung und teils auch Neubewertung des Status quo"*. The target reader is the *fachkundige Leser*.
- **BWING24 rule:** **4 of 6 TFs must be in a technical module** (Formale Anforderungen; FAQ "Technische Transferleistungen: BWING"). See [technical_focus_bwing24.md](technical_focus_bwing24.md).

## Timeline (FAQ "Wie lange dauert…")

```
Auftragsklärung upload (CIS)
   └─ ≤ 1 week (Werktage): NAK examiner checks → accepted | rejected with reason (revise & resubmit, repeatable)
        └─ accepted → 4-week Bearbeitungszeitraum starts ("Die Erstellung der Arbeit beginnt mit Freigabe der Auftragsklärung")
             └─ upload TF + Selbstreflexion + Eigenständigkeitserklärung (+ KI-Prozessdokumentation) by 23:59 of deadline day
                  └─ ≤ 4 weeks examiner grading → Team Transferleistungen check/release (up to ~4 more days)
Ideal total ≈ 9 weeks.
```

- **Max. 2 TFs in progress in CIS at once.**
- No minimum working time: early submission is allowed.
- **No changes after upload.** The upload notifies the examiner and starts their 4-week grading period. *"Sie können keine Änderungen mehr vornehmen, sobald Datei hochgeladen"* (Formale Anforderungen).
- **Not submitted after 4 weeks → failed**, and you need a new TF with a new topic.
- **Illness:** you need a *Prüfungsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung* (an AU is no longer enough according to the PVO), sent to the Team Transferleistungen. The deadline is extended by the certified days.
- **Interviews belong inside the 4 weeks.** Contacting interviewees beforehand is fine.

## Submission mechanics (FAQ "Dokumente", "Wie überprüfe ich…")

- Upload **three files** to CIS: **Transferleistung**, **Selbstreflexion**, **Eigenständigkeitserklärung**. If AI was used, also the **KI-Prozessdokumentation** (FAQ "Einsatz von KI"). Formale Anforderungen says *"Prozessdokumentation über KI-Nutzung anfügen"*. See [ai_usage.md](ai_usage.md).
- The TF is **one PDF including the appendix, max 10 MB**. Separate appendix files are not possible. The examiner sees only the TF document and the Selbstreflexion. Links to further documents are allowed.
- Afterwards check that the status changes from *"Erstellungszeit läuft"* to *"abgegeben"*, and take a screenshot. A missing mandatory field (e.g. workload) silently blocks submission. If there is an error message, email the documents plus a screenshot to transferleistungen@nordakademie.de **before the deadline**.
- An empty or missing Selbstreflexion means the **TF is failed** (FAQ "Fließen die Auftragsklärung…").

## Anonymity / Double-blind

- Neither side knows the other. **Your name must not appear** in the TF, on the Deckblatt, in interview protocols or in translation notes (use "Übersetzung Autor"). Use the official Deckblatt ([2022_07_Dokument_Transferleistung_Deckblatt.pdf](<../originals/2022_07_Dokument_Transferleistung_Deckblatt.pdf>)). Its fields are: *Transferleistung Theorie/Praxis Nr., Matrikelnummer, Freigegebenes Thema, Studiengang, Zenturie*.
- The Eigenständigkeitserklärung is uploaded separately. **Do not** put it into the TF document.

## Sperrvermerk (FAQ "Sperrvermerk")

- You set it as a checkbox when uploading the **Auftragsklärung** in CIS. It is binding. Only the examination office and the examiner have access, and both are under confidentiality clauses. Examiners may only open TFs in the browser.
- **Do not add a separate Sperrvermerk page** to the document (double-blind). If the company insists, the betrieblicher Betreuer signs it.
- If the company worries: showing only excerpts, redacting, or leaving data out of the appendix are all allowed, with a note in the text. Verifiability still matters: data needed for the analysis should be in the appendix.
- An examiner comment: a Sperrvermerk hides your results from interested readers, so think about whether you really need it.

## Examiner & supervisor roles

- **NAK examiner (Prüfer:in):** you cannot choose them. They are experts in the field. There is no direct contact; go via the Team Transferleistungen. Comments on the Auftragsklärung in CIS give important hints. No comment means the examiner agrees with the plan.
- **Betrieblicher Betreuer:** needs expertise in the field, interest and willingness to support you. No degree is required. Tasks: work out the Auftragsklärung with you and sign it (Freigabe), help rephrase after a rejection, coach you, give feedback. NAK does not ask the Betreuer for an assessment. If the Betreuer drops out, contact the Ausbildungsleitung.
- **Acceptance criteria for the topic:** does it fit the chosen module, is the question or problem clearly described, and does the planned approach look coherent and goal-oriented (FAQ "Nach welchen Kriterien…")?

## Modules

- Pick a module from the **previous or current semester** (recommended). A future module is allowed if the company needs it, but you must teach yourself the content.
- A module can be chosen for several (even all 6) TFs. A topic spanning two modules: free choice. Wahlpflichtmodule use the placeholder "WPM" plus an email with the topic's thematic classification to transferleistungen@nordakademie.de.
- Topic ideas: the Modulhandbuch, section *"Verflechtung mit der betrieblichen Praxis"*, for each module. Also the company's Rahmenplan.
- One big topic split into **two TFs** is possible if both parts are fully independent, each meets all requirements, and each has its own Auftragsklärung. There may be different examiners. No copy-paste between them.

## Team projects (PVO 2024, §§ 18, 29 Abs. 1)

Several students may work on one company project together, but **each writes and submits an individual TF** showing their own contribution, results and reflection. Grading is individual. **State in the Selbstreflexion whether the project was teamwork.**

## Failing, repeats, BA thesis

- A failed TF can be repeated **twice, with new topics**. Failing those as well means **Exmatrikulation**. Repeats may use a different module.
- If you disagree with a grade: ask the examiner for an explanation via the Team; a *Widerspruchsverfahren* before the Prüfungsausschuss is possible.
- Feedback: you get grades per criterion. Failed criteria come with an explanation.
- **BA thesis registration requires TFs 1–5 passed**, including any repeat attempts. What counts is the sequence 1–5, not the count.
- **TF 6** must be submitted **at the latest together with the BA thesis** (PVO §15 (3)). Late submission means the BA thesis is not passed. It may be a *Vorstudie* to the thesis (mention this under "Wie lautet Ihr Thema"). Same process and criteria. It must be self-contained. You must pick a concrete module because "Bachelorarbeit" is not selectable. Its examiner is different from the thesis examiner. If it is a Vorstudie, email it as a PDF to the thesis examiner.

## Self-plagiarism & reuse

- **Reusing passages word for word from earlier graded papers or TFs is taboo** and counts as an attempt to deceive (Täuschungsversuch). Reusing knowledge, sources and results with references is fine. Earlier work can be attached in the appendix and cited (e.g. *"Anhang X, Transferleistung 6, S. xx"*). Exception: short low-effort passages such as a company profile may be copied.

## English

TFs may be written in English, using the English forms (Moodle checkbox "Materials in englisch"). A German TF in an English-language module is fine if the title makes the language clear.
