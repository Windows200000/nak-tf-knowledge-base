### ⚠ Discrepancy: Auftragsklärung length

- The **Auftragsklärung form itself** ([ab 2024](<../originals/Auftragsklärung_Transferleistung ab 2024.pdf>) and [BWING24 version](<../originals/2024_08 BWING24__Auftragsklärung_Transferleistung.pdf>)) says: *"Insgesamt stehen Ihnen als Richtwert **3000 Zeichen** zur Verfügung."*
- **2026_Formale Anforderungen**, **Tipps (2022)** and the **FAQ** say: *"Auftragsklärung: **2000 Zeichen** (Richtwert)"*.

Both are guide values (*Richtwert*). The form is the document actually submitted and was updated for 2024, so 3000 is the binding upper guide. Aiming for **≈ 2000–2500 characters** satisfies both. When in doubt, ask transferleistungen@nordakademie.de.

## Standard chapter structure (Tipps p. 3)

1. **Einleitung**: Motivation, problem-oriented lead-in, overview of the paper. Examiner version: *Hintergrund, Zielsetzung, Gang der Untersuchung*. It is chapter **1**, with no extra title.
2. **Grundlagen / Konzepte / Theorie**: explanation, problem description, literature review.
3. **Literaturreview**: state of research → research gap → **Forschungsfrage**.
4. **Entwurf eines Modells (Hypothese)**.
5. **Überprüfung des Modells**: Proof-of-Concept, empirical work etc. (data collection, evaluation, results); analysis and solution.
6. **Beurteilung / Darstellung der Ergebnisse**.
7. **Zusammenfassung / Handlungsempfehlung / Fazit / ggf. Ausblick**: the key results, summarised pointedly and critically; open questions; further research or follow-up measures.

Examiner comments add: **a dedicated method chapter** early, right after the introduction (see [reviewer_comments.md](reviewer_comments.md)). For a 10-page paper, combine items 2+3 and 4–6 sensibly.

## Outline (Gliederung) rules

- **"Wer A sagt, muss auch B sagen"**: never a single sub-point on a level. ✗ `1, 2, 2.1, 3` ✓ `1, 2, 3, 4, 4.1, 4.2` (Tipps p. 3).
- Keep the roter Faden. The goal must not be scattered across headings. The conclusion is one chapter, not three.
- Text under every heading level is not wrong, but must be **consistent**. For short TFs NAK recommends **text only on the lowest level**. Avoid one-sentence filler under top-level headings (FAQ).
- New page per main chapter: free choice. For short TFs it is usually not recommended (FAQ).

## Language & style

- **No Ich/Wir form** and no colloquial language in the TF (Tipps p. 2; examiner comments). (The Selbstreflexion is the exception: Ich-Form is wanted.)
- Objectivity: *"nichts ist selbstverständlich, natürlich, klar, offensichtlich"*. Consider opposing viewpoints.
- Give the company name **with its legal form** (*Firmierung*), or introduce an abbreviation.
- **Gendering** (FAQ): no mandated form. Pick one style and use it **consistently**. Readability comes first. Neutral collective terms are suggested (Führungskraft instead of Manager). A disclaimer for the generic masculine is possible but increasingly criticised.
- **Foreign-language quotes** (FAQ): English quotes are not translated. Other languages are translated in [brackets] marked *"Übersetzung Autor"* (no name, because of double-blind). Use direct quotes very sparingly.

## Citation basics that are NAK-specific

- Every non-common-knowledge statement needs a source. **Internal sources must be verifiable**: put them in the appendix and cite them from there.
- One footnote at the end of a sentence covers only that sentence. For several consecutive sentences from one source, mark it, e.g. *"Vgl. (auch im Folgenden)…"*, which ends with the paragraph (Tipps p. 4).
- Modified figures need a source reference. Own figures need no "eigene Darstellung" note (Tipps p. 4). Other guides differ, so be consistent.
- **E-books** are cited like books, with no "E-Book" note (FAQ).
- **Lecture slides and scripts** are grey literature and *not citable*. Use them only to find real sources. If unavoidable, add a footnote referencing the lecture (date, lecturer, module) and ask the lecturer for permission (FAQ).
- Style choice: whichever is standard in the field, used consistently. Style guides in this repo: [citation/](citation/README.md).

## Templates

NAK provides **no official Word or LaTeX template** on purpose (FAQ). Student LaTeX templates exist in NAK GitLab and Overleaf. This dump contains a student-made **Typst template**; see [typst_template.md](typst_template.md). Its defaults deviate from some of the numbers above.
